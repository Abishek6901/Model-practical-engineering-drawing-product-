# Model-practical-engineering-drawing-product.
DESIGN AND FABRICATION OF A FOLDABLE LAPTOP TABLE
USING WPVC MATERIAL
Objectives
•
The primary objectives of this project are:
1.To design a foldable laptop table that is both simple and functional.
2.To use WPVC (Wood Polymer Composite) as the primary construction material. 2.1 Existing Laptop Tables
A variety of laptop tables are available in the market. Common types include:
Wooden Bed Trays: Classic tables with fixed or folding legs.
Metal Laptop Stands: Often portable but may lack a large, stable work surface.
Plastic Molded Tables: Can be light but may not be very strong or aesthetically
pleasing.
•MDF/Plywood Tables: The most common type, offering a balance of cost and
strength but often heavy.
2.2 Limitations of Existing Designs
•High Cost: Well-designed, sturdy tables from reputable brands can be expensive.
•Heavy Construction: Wooden and metal tables are often heavy, making them
difficult to move.
•Limited Portability: Bulky designs are not travel-friendly.
3.To reduce the overall weight and manufacturing cost compared to existing
products.
4.To provide a design that allows for easy folding and storage.
5.To improve portability and usability for the end-user.
1.5 Scope of the Project
This project encompasses the complete cycle from material selection and design
to the fabrication and testing of a functional prototype. The scope includes
creating detailed design drawings, sourcing the necessary materials,
manufacturing the table, and evaluating its performance. The final product is
